THIS vs. THAT

Yo Reader,

Have a Short idea that you REALLY like, but it didn't perform well?

Just change the premise to "This vs. That"

Example: classic "starbucks is making too much money" video

**OLD Concept:** "Starbucks is making too much money!"

What we did: Go to Starbucks, talk about it outside while buying the drink.

Result? Honestly, not too shabby.

**So We Pivoted**

We thought through the lens of "This Vs. That"

We made a video using an already viral LinkedIn Post as a base and had Gary go into Starbucks, break down the total cost, and use a stronger hook.

**Final Result?** Easy views.
