Results Matter

Yo Reader!

I cannot express the amount of times I've heard people ask this question:

"Do you know what the definition of 'Insanity' is?"

They always have that stupid, smug face.

We all know the answer:

"Doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results"

Oh yeah? Well guess what... I just looked it up — that's actually nowhere near Einstein's real quote.

Anyway.

Most of you listened to Gary Vee who said "Post 10x a DAY and you will blow up."

Honestly, it wasn't terrible advice.

But most people went out and paid someone to make 90 videos a month for them and post the SAME EXACT video 90x a month and then act confused when one of them doesn't blow up.

You will not get millions of views from posting 100 videos. 100 videos will teach you HOW to get millions of views.

So yeah, are you insane for posting the same talking head video and expecting to get millions of views?

100%

That's why you need to add layers.

Dino
