Longform Tutorials Are Dead

Yo Reader!

Let me back track a bit... because this isn't necessarily true.

But it's pretty darn close.

The other day I was ATTEMPTING to use a Breville Espresso Machine. But I had no idea how to use it... So I went to YouTube.

And a few options popped up:

- A "how to" video that was nearly 10 min long
- And a Short (less than 60 seconds).

Which one do you think I watched?

**Shorts Can Be Evergreen**

They don't have to be quick pops. I've had plenty of videos get longtail views YEARS after I post them.

So think through some tutorials your audience may be searching for and find a way to teach it in 60 seconds.

It could EASILY help you rank highly in YouTube/Google search results (since no one is really talking about this right now).
