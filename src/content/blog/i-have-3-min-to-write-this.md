Sometimes the Best Videos are The Worst Videos...

Yo!

You ever spend hours editing a Short and it gets 500 views MAX... Then you post a random video that you don't care about and it EXPLODES???

Yeah, that's super frustrating (I get it).

It's currently 2:44pm and I'm late to writing this. My wife and I have to leave in order to close on our new house(!!!!) but I NEED to send this out to you all.

*Why?*

Because sometimes the best stuff we can make is when we are short on time.

***WHY?***

Because when we have deadlines, ***our expectations decline DRASTICALLY***, and when we have low expectations, we can have a little bit less stress and anxiety when posting a video.

And when you ***stop making "to optimize for the algo" and start posting "for fun"...***

That's when some of your BEST work is going to come out.
