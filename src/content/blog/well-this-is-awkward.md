Here's three big takeaways to help you find MASSIVE growth with your Shorts:

**Make Formats**

In a sea of white dudes sitting behind a desk, be James Cordon instead of a Stephen Colbert.

Cordon gave us ICONIC video series where Colbert just does the same talking head bit every time.

Make multiple formats.

**Perfect Your "First Frame"**

Jenny Hoyos is the best at this.

View your first FRAME of the short video as your Thumbnail and make it CLEAR.

**Simplify Your Speech**

Use hemingwayapp.com to figure out what reading level your video's script is at.

Odds are your corporate business jargon reads at a 12th+ grade.

Humans prefer reading at a 5th grade level... so figure out how to get it there.
