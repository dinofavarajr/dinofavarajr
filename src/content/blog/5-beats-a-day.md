Quantity vs. Quality

Yo Reader!

idk if you remember but GaryVee once said to post 100x a day.

That is wild (and nearly impossible for just one person).

**Here's the Deal:**

People would rather find someone with a back catalogue of 20 GOOD videos than someone with 100 OKAY videos (at least, that's just me).

But I want to answer this question: Can you jumpstart an account through quantity? And if so, how?

**The Short Answer:** ...maybe

Let's be clear, the only way to post MULTIPLE times and it be effective is to do it with one strategy in mind: **Testing.**

Each video should be an "at bat" to test to see what works, and what doesn't.

Accounts fail when they look at the number of posts as a checklist.

**Here's the formula:**

1. Test 20+ DIFFERENT formats and premises
2. Analyze what performed best
3. Make 20+ more variations of best performers
4. Streamline and automate the winners
5. Repeat

That's it.

Do this every week and you can't lose.
