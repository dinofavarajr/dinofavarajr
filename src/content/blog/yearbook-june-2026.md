## Behind the Scenes — Find People Who Believe in You

I have been listening to a biography on the life and business of Walt Disney, and one big takeaway comes from his first failed business: *Laugh-O-Gram Studio.*

Although it filed for bankruptcy in 1923 after only two years in business, something stuck with me on the lesson of getting something off the ground.

When Walt was first getting animation gigs, he was an absolute nobody in the middle of nowhere Kansas. Most gigs paid very little or nothing at all. He knew he could not build a studio without a team of animators, so he started recruiting. He had almost no money to pay them — the only compensation he could offer was ACCESS to do work and get experience. So they did.

He formed a small team of young men from around town and they created their own little "animation fraternity." They moved quick, spent all of their time together, and sometimes even *slept* in the studio.

This blew my mind. This man had no money and STILL was able to build a team to chase a dream.

So that's what I tried to do too... and it worked.

I connected with four different friends who were looking for some creative editing and production work. I broke down my video production into repeatable systems for each to own, and I offered them a small base pay plus a percentage of video ad revenue.

The biggest thing? When I asked why they were interested, all of them wanted something I had to offer: **Opportunity.**

- The opportunity to try a new editing style.
- The opportunity to get experience on a production team.
- The opportunity to be a part of a budding YouTube channel.

> **Opportunity**: "A favorable juncture of circumstances."

I believe the only difference between where I am now and being full time is just 50 more videos. With this crew I'm assembling, it's inevitable — and I haven't been this excited in a while.

*(I will still be doing a final polish of each video with a heavy, hands-on approach to the edit. This will severely shorten the amount of time I'm spending in Adobe Premiere and allow more time for planning and filming more videos.)*
