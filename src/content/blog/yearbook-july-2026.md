Whaddup Fam. Happy July!

As some of you know, this month we are celebrating America's Semiquincentennial birthday. I'm writing this newsletter in a hotel room in DC while finishing up the final touches on the next video.

Hopefully you are celebrating with your friends and family in a restful way this weekend. Jenny and I are taking the kids to the beach — our first big vacation as a family — so we're pumped.

LOTS to share about the month of June. Some good, some sad. But overall, there is SO much momentum it's crazy.

## Behind the Scenes

15 days ago I was let go of my role at Sweet Fish Media due to market conditions.

Obviously — huge bummer. I would have LOVED to have this change happen on my own terms. However, I cannot tell this story without what happened that morning.

I was on my walk and just asked the Lord with open hands: *"What do you have for me here?"*

I knew there was something bigger He was calling me into and I've been able to feel it for a while. I just didn't know what it was. I genuinely was ready to hear from Him.

And 4 hours later I got an answer.

The craziest part about this? I'm not worried.

Why? Because a TON of friends and family have come around me and supported me, and I feel the Lord is calling me into this season. I literally walk with Him every morning around a lake near my house and just try to listen to what He has for me. Not only that, but opportunities are seemingly popping up out of the wazoo — work connections, business conversations, job opportunities, and so many more.

My plan for the next 30 days is to do a full analysis of the options in front of me and choose what's best for my family during this season. A few I've been wrestling with:

- Rebuilding my marketing agency, [Mercury Media](https://www.mercurysocialmedia.com/), that I did full time from 2021–2024
- Pursuing two full time job opportunities that are in front of me to lead their marketing and social team
- YOLO-ing and just doing YouTube full time

That last option isn't viable right now, but I believe with an updated posting cadence and a weekly rhythm of reaching out to brands, this could be very achievable in the next 6 months.

Through this, I appreciate YOUR support. I've been thinking about how to write this without it reading like a "poor me" and instead more like a "check out this new opportunity to grow the channel."

The wildest part is that I'd scheduled our big kickoff call weeks ago, and it happened to fall the literal day *after* I got the news. Talk about a perfect segue into this next stage. Coincidence? I think not.

## What's On My Mind

No Comments of the Month this go around, but in the meantime I'd like to share some things I've been working through. Maybe you're in a similar season. Maybe not. But if you've been looking for book, music, or media recs — hopefully this is interesting to you.

**Books**
- *The Book of Joshua* in the Bible. Homeboy stepped into leadership of Israel after Moses died and he truly had to let the Lord guide his steps. Lots of lessons I'm learning from this.
- *The Alchemist* by Paulo Coelho. I'm re-reading it but it's such a banger if you've ever wanted to DO something.

**Music**
- My All-Time Top Songs on Spotify. No AI was used in creating this playlist — just raw amount of plays. Everything in order from most listened to minimum, plus my first stream ever on the account. It takes me through every season of life.
- *10,000 Reasons* by Matt Redman. Oldie but a goodie. Have the CD at home from when I bought it my senior year of high school and I can just listen to the whole thing without worrying about a stray Maverick City Music song jump scaring me during my quiet time.

**Media**
- A video by Ali Gallop that I'd encourage you to just put on the TV with an open mind. Unironically I would love to create a video like this one day.
- Cinematic dancing to Michael Jackson's popular music. I watched one of these close to 40 times already and now that's all I see — which I'm totally fine with.
