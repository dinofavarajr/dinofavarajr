You Are Always One Video Away

Yo Reader!

Something I love doing: watching the video I posted RIGHT BEFORE another one I made popped off.

I remember posting those videos and how I felt...

I felt like I didn't know what I was doing. I was frustrated. I was confused. Why do some people always seem to do well? Why do I feel like I'll never have a successful video even when I'm constantly obsessing over this?

Christmas 2022 — I post two videos with the same expectation:

"I hope this one is the banger"

One took off, and the other didn't. These were about a week apart and I was about ready to give up on posting to this TikTok account.

In one of these I'm trying to do what everyone else says you should do... while in the other I am letting my creativity flow and having FUN with new concepts.

**You Could Be 1 Video Away from Success and You Have No Idea**

If I look at the guy filming both of these videos, I can see a dude who has no idea what's to come. He has no idea that each little "pop" brings a new set of eyes, a new set of followers, and (more importantly) a new epiphany for further growth.

I believe the journey of the content is much less of "post 10x a day" and more like building a house...

The house looks terrible... until it doesn't anymore.

When it's just the wood, it looks terrible. When it's the concrete slab, it looks terrible. When it has walls but no paint, it looks terrible.

Until one day the final touch is made and it looks like a beautiful house.

View yourself as building a digital structure and it will make posting videos a whole lot more fun and enjoyable.
