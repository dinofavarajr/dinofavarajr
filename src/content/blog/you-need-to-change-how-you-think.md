Yo Reader!

Yesterday I talked about Preston and how he got 100k Subscribers from ONE Short. Pretty crazy.

But today I want to show you how YOU can get a result like this too.

**Step 1: Be Hot (optional)**

Are you hot? Well, do I have news for you... this is the last step in the process for you. All you have to do is start posting and then you should be good to go!

**Step 2: Think Broadly**

Okay, so if you're reading this, I'm assuming you are not hot (honestly, same).

We gotta think differently here!

The first thing you're going to do is think of your idea... what do you want to make?

- Funny skit
- Trending topic
- Challenge concept

Let's say you want to make a video in the Finance/Business niche. You wanna talk about BIDNESS!

Okay that's great... but no one really cares about business (I know you might, but MAJORITY of people don't). So let's think about what TOPICS people are interested in that can lead them to be interested in business.

For example:

- DON'T - Make a video about how to optimize cash flow
- DO - Make a video about STARBUCKS (everyone knows about Starbucks) and how THEY optimize cash flow

See the difference here?

Okay so now we know what we're going to make: A video about how Starbucks organizes their cash flow.

But is it good enough? Do we just sit down in front of the camera and talk about it?

NO

We're gonna go one step further and add some layers to it.

These layers will transform your video from a "900 view Nancy" and into an irresistible piece of content your viewer just gobbles up.

And we'll talk about how to add layers... tomorrow (;
